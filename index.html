WHO WE ARE

A UNION BUILT FOR THE GAME.

The Union of Liberian Footballers is a not-for-profit organization established to protect and promote the shared interests of current and former football participants in Liberia.

Its constitutional objectives emphasize unity, dignity, welfare, professional growth, integrity, and accountability.

Discover Our Purpose
OUR PURPOSE

MORE THAN A FOOTBALL UNION.

The Union's constitution outlines objectives focused on protecting members, supporting professional development, and contributing to football in Liberia.

01

Rights & Representation

Promoting the rights, welfare, and professional interests of current and former Liberian footballers.

02

Member Welfare

Supporting objectives relating to social, medical, educational, pension, and emergency assistance.

03

Career Development

Encouraging training, career transition, networking, and professional growth.

04

Football Development

Contributing to grassroots football and community engagement.

05

Good Governance

Promoting transparency, accountability, integrity, and responsible leadership.

STRUCTURE & GOVERNANCE

BUILT ON ACCOUNTABILITY.

The constitution establishes structures for oversight, administration, and member participation.

01 / DECISION-MAKING

General Assembly

The highest decision-making body of the Union.

02 / OVERSIGHT

Advisory Board

An elected body responsible for oversight.

03 / ADMINISTRATION

Executive Management

The administrative structure of the Union.

04 / SPECIALIZED WORK

Standing Committees

Committees covering welfare, finance, membership, elections, ethics, and records.

05 / LOCAL STRUCTURES

Local Chapters

Local structures recognized in the governance framework.
